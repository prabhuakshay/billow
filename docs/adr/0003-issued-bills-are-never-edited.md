# Issued Bills are never edited

Once a Bill is issued, Billow never changes it, not even to fix a misspelt name or a wrong address. A mistake is fixed by cancelling the Bill (it keeps its number and is marked cancelled) and issuing a new one. GST law does not allow a Tax Invoice to be altered after issue, and a Bill already reported in a filed GSTR-1 must match what was reported.

## Considered Options

- **Let someone edit the Customer details on an issued Bill, but not its amounts.** Rejected: it is common in small shops but not lawful, and it silently breaks Bills already reported in a filed return.
- **Credit and Debit Notes.** These are the lawful way to correct amounts or tax on a Bill from an earlier period. They are not ruled out, only deferred until Bills exist.
