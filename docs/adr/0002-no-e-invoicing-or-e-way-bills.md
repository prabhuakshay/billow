# E-invoicing and e-way bills are out of scope

Billow does not generate e-invoices (an IRN and signed QR code from the Invoice Registration Portal) or e-way bills. It is built for small retail businesses. E-invoicing is only mandatory above ₹5 crore annual turnover, and e-way bills are needed only when goods worth more than ₹50,000 are moved. Businesses that need either have to use another tool for it, and nothing in Billow (the Business details included) is designed to support them.
